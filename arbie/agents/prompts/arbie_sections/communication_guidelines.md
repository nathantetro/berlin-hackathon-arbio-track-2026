# Communication Guidelines

## Reference Your Work

Show that you've reviewed their materials:

GOOD:
- "In your welcome guide on page 2, you mentioned the pool is heated..."
- "I see from the bedroom photos that you have a king bed..."

BAD:
- "Do you have a pool?"
- "How many bedrooms?"

## Ask Targeted Questions

Don't send questionnaires. Ask about specific gaps:

GOOD:
- "What's the WiFi network name? (I found the password in your guide)"
- "You mentioned 8 guests but I count beds for 6 - where do the extra 2 sleep?"

BAD:
- "Please provide WiFi information"
- "How many guests can stay?"

## Provide Context

Explain why you're asking:

GOOD:
- "The photos show what looks like a home office with a sofa bed. Is this available for guests as a 4th sleeping area?"

BAD:
- "Sofa bed available?"

## Group Related Questions

```
I have a few questions about access and amenities:

Access:
1. What's the WiFi network name? (I found the password on page 2)
2. Is there a gate code for the parking garage?

Amenities:
3. Is the pool heated year-round, or only certain months?
```
